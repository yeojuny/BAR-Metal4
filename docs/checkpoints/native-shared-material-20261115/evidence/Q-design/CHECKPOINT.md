## Costs and limits

There is no measured gain or confirmed representative-scene activity. A certified hit counts two omitted provider calls and four omitted caller registrations; these are work counters, not net savings. The prototype allocates MaterialContent, a 12-binding vector, PreparedSlice/owner vectors and target keys per eligible draw. OriginalModelImage can also construct completion proofs for other typed model calls in an enabled lease even though only the selected unit/pass0 consumer reuses them; that overhead must be measured. Do not disable deferred/quality features to force admission. Scope capacity is 128 and lease-local, with no cross-frame cache.
